# A Lean proof of c₂ ≥ 1.7027237335 for the centred maximal operator over squares

Let c₂ be the weak type (1, 1) constant of the centred Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator over axis-parallel
squares in the plane: the least C with α |{M f > α}| ≤ C ‖f‖₁ for every integrable f and every level α. This
project proves, in Lean 4 with Mathlib,

```lean
theorem CenteredMaximal.ofReal_le_weakTypeConstant_two :
    ENNReal.ofReal (17027237335 / 10000000000) ≤ weakTypeConstant 2
```

and its exact form `ofReal_le_weakTypeConstant_two_exact`, with the rational number
482167717377842991882283179477574801392641890014291050323 / 283174368139841919615520431313000857449005056000000000000
= 1.70272373359629… in place of 1.7027237335. The definitions of the maximal function, of a weak type bound and
of `weakTypeConstant` are Yongxi Lin's, unchanged (`CenteredMaximal/Statement.lean`). The previous lower bound
was Lin's c₂ ≥ 1.68550999… (also proved in Lean, in the repository credited below).

Both theorems depend only on the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. The certificate is
checked by `decide +kernel`, so no `native_decide` and no `Lean.ofReduceBool` are used anywhere.

## How the proof works

1. *Periodic configurations* (`CenteredMaximal/Periodic/Witness.lean`, `Smear.lean`, `LowerBound.lean`). A
   periodic configuration of point masses has periods px, py and finitely many atoms per period cell. A level-one
   witness for a point z is a side L ≥ ℓ together with a finite set of atoms of total mass at least L², all within
   sup distance L/2 of z, where ℓ > 0 is a fixed lower bound for the sides. Suppose every point of a measurable
   subset G of the period cell [0, px) × [0, py) has a witness that uses atoms of the cell and of its eight
   neighbours. Then |G| / (mass of a period cell) ≤ c₂ (`Config.ofReal_div_le_weakTypeConstant`). The proof is
   Lin's: truncate the configuration to (2N + 3)² cells, smear each atom over a small square, apply the weak type
   inequality to the (2N + 1)² disjoint translates of G, and let N → ∞. Lin's lattice is replaced by an arbitrary
   periodic configuration, and his witness side bound 1 by ℓ.
2. *The certificate* (`Cert.lean`, `CertSound.lean`). A certificate gives the configuration as exact natural
   numbers (coordinates in units 1/Q, masses in units 1/Dm). It lists witness rectangles, each with a radius ρ
   and an explicit list of distinct atoms of total mass at least 4ρ², all within distance ρ of every point of
   the rectangle. It also lists half-open pieces, each inside one witness rectangle, in an order that makes
   them pairwise disjoint. G is the union of the pieces. `checkCert` checks all of this, and that 2ρ ≥ ℓ.
   Atom codes can only name atoms of the cell and of its eight neighbours, so every witness the checker accepts
   uses neighbouring atoms only. `checkCert` uses only natural-number arithmetic. `Cert.ofReal_le_weakTypeConstant`
   proves that a certificate passing `checkCert c rNum rDen` gives rNum / rDen ≤ c₂.
3. *The data* (`Data.lean` and `Data/*.lean`, generated by `tools/gen_cert.py` from
   `data/c2lower_1p70272_e2ca1616.json`). The
   configuration has 50 atoms per period cell: masses 1 and about 4.008 on alternating columns (Lin's frame),
   and two horizontal lines of 6 equally spaced small atoms, copied by the frame's mirror symmetries. The
   certificate has 405 witness rectangles (11,396 atom references, radii on the grid 2⁻⁴⁰ · ℤ, side bound
   ℓ = 1/16) and 1,556 pieces. `Main.lean` runs `checkCert` in the kernel (about 5 s per check) and concludes.

## Building

Requirements: `elan` (the toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0-rc3` is pinned in `lean-toolchain`) and `git`.

```
lake exe cache get    # downloads the Mathlib build for revision c55e6e786f49471c72fbddbec5415808896aec1e
lake build            # builds the 15 modules of CenteredMaximal
lake env lean Axioms.lean
```

The last command prints the axioms of the two theorems. With the Mathlib build in place, `lake build` takes
about 25 s, of which about 10 s are the two kernel checks in `Main.lean`.

To regenerate the certificate data, run
`python3 -I tools/gen_cert.py data/c2lower_1p70272_e2ca1616.json OUTDIR`. This writes `OUTDIR/Data.lean` and
`OUTDIR/Data/*.lean`, byte-identical to `CenteredMaximal/Periodic/Data.lean` and `CenteredMaximal/Periodic/Data/`,
and `OUTDIR/cert.json`. The script also checks the data with a Python copy of `checkCert`. The Lean proof does not
depend on the script: the kernel checks the data itself.
